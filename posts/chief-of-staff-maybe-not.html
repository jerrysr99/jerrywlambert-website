Chief Of Staff? Maybe Not

September 25, 2026

I have been running a lot of analysis lately on how tokens are almost invisibly passed between agents in a team. And the effect of the teaming structure on token consumption. In Grokbot, for example, the tokens that are routed internally between the agents use a model called grok-bot-default. 54% of the tokens I burned in GrokBot in the last 30 days (2.59B tokens) were consumed with the agents talking to each other. Big.

When I drill down on that more closely, the biggest spend is the back and forth to and from the Chief Of Staff who is staying in the loop of everything. A COS is good organizationally, but not good for efficiency. You need to design your team member personas so that they have more autonomy to perform without approvals and collaborations where it is possible.

Every time an agent wakes up the COS it dumps a context in. Every time the COS wakes up an agent it dumps a context in. Agents operating with no budget and no real concern for bloat.

Be careful. Watch your budgets. Analyze your spend. See below.
