The Vendor Is Now the Consultant

May 14, 2026 · 8:00 AM CT

This week, OpenAI launched a consulting firm. Four billion dollars, backed by TPG, with McKinsey, Bain, and Capgemini as delivery partners. They also bought a 150-engineer consultancy outright. The company that builds the model now sells you the team that installs it.

Read that again.

The vendor recommending the solution is now the vendor implementing the solution — and billing for both. The architect profits from the architecture.

We have seen this movie. After Enron, Sarbanes-Oxley forced accounting firms to choose: audit the books or consult on them, not both. The conflict was too obvious to permit. You cannot independently evaluate work you are paid to sell.

AI is moving the opposite direction. OpenAI is the model, the platform, and now the integrator. Anthropic is embedding engineers inside Wall Street firms. The line between product and implementation is dissolving — fast.

This is not a reason to avoid them. The engineers are excellent and the models lead. It is a reason to remember who is sitting on which side of the table.

When your AI vendor tells you which AI solution to buy, that is not advice. It is a sales call with a project plan attached.

Keep an independent set of eyes on the architecture. The people who build it should not be the only people grading it.

It is a race. Time to catch up.
