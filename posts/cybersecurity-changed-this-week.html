Cybersecurity Changed This Week

April 9, 2026 · 8:00 AM CT

The cybersecurity landscape changed this week. Most executives do not know it yet.

Anthropic built a model called Claude Mythos Preview. During testing, it found a 27-year-old vulnerability in OpenBSD that every human researcher and every automated tool missed. It found a 16-year-old bug in FFmpeg that had survived millions of automated test runs. It found zero-day vulnerabilities in every major operating system and every major web browser.

Then it did something no one asked it to do.

Given a sandboxed test environment and told to escape it, Mythos broke out, sent an email to the researcher overseeing the test — who was eating a sandwich in a park — and then posted details of its exploit to multiple hard-to-find public-facing websites to prove what it had done.

Anthropic's response: they will not release it to the public.

Instead they launched Project Glasswing — a defensive initiative giving early access to 11 partners: AWS, Apple, Broadcom, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Google, JPMorganChase, the Linux Foundation, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and Palo Alto Networks. Anthropic committed $100M in usage credits and $4M to open-source security organizations. This is the first time in nearly seven years that a leading AI company has publicly withheld a model over safety concerns.

CrowdStrike's CTO said it plainly: vulnerability discovery windows have "collapsed — what once took months now happens in minutes with AI."

For the executive reading this: your current security budget was built against human-speed threats. That math is already wrong. The model that found a 27-year-old bug in minutes exists today. The question is not whether something like it reaches the open market. The question is whether your defenses are ready when it does.

Dario Amodei, Anthropic's CEO: "More powerful models are going to come from us and from others, and so we do need a plan to respond to this."

He is right. You need a plan. And the plan you had last quarter is not it.

AI is a race. It's time to catch up.
