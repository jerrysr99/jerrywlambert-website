The Agent Layer Goes Live

April 16, 2026 · 8:00 AM CT

Enterprises have been debating AI agents for two years. This was the week four vendors turned them into something usable...native.

  • Anthropic — Managed Agents entered public beta at $0.08 per session-hour. Notion, Asana, and Sentry are already building on it.
  • Microsoft — Copilot Studio multi-agent orchestration hit general availability, running over the open Agent-to-Agent protocol across 15 million+ M365 Copilot seats.
  • OpenAI — Codex, its coding agent, scaled from near-zero at the start of the quarter to three million users. Two million developers use it weekly; 6x growth since January.
  • xAI — Grok Computer, an autonomous desktop agent, entered beta. It clicks buttons, fills forms, and navigates applications on its own.

Four companies. One week.

An agent is not a feature inside your software. An agent is the employee that uses the software. It logs in. It opens the tab. It reads the invoice, files the ticket, posts the message, closes the loop — at three in the morning, billed by the session-hour.

That is a workforce decision, not an IT purchase. And somewhere inside your company, a developer already authorized the first one. The meter is running whether the CFO signed off or not.

It is a race. Time to catch up.
