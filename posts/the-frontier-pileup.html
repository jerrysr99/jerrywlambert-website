The Frontier Pileup

April 24, 2026 · 8:00 AM CT

GPT-5.5. DeepSeek V4. Tencent Hy3. Three frontier models. Forty-eight hours. The benchmarks are separated by single-digit percentage points.

That is the story.

The model race did not produce a winner. It produced a tie. And a tie changes the entire enterprise conversation.

When every model scores within two points on the coding benchmarks that matter — SWE-bench, LiveCodeBench, Terminal-Bench — the question stops being "which model is smartest." It becomes:

Where does it run? DeepSeek V4 ships under an MIT license — more permissive than Apache 2.0 — at frontier-class performance. Self-host it anywhere. Your data never leaves your network. GPT-5.5 lives in OpenAI's cloud. Hy3 is open-weight under Apache 2.0.

Who built it? Two of the three came from China. DeepSeek trained entirely on Huawei chips, outside NVIDIA's CUDA ecosystem. Jensen Huang called that outcome "horrible for our nation."

What does it cost? DeepSeek V4-Pro runs $1.74 per million tokens. GPT-5.5 runs $5. That is not a rounding error.

The capability gap closed. The trust gap did not. The price gap widened.

And here is the part no one is saying out loud: an MIT license solves the data sovereignty problem. It does not solve the model integrity problem. Security researchers have documented vulnerabilities in DeepSeek's weights that persist regardless of where you host it. The model is banned from government devices in Texas, New York, Virginia, and Florida. Italy banned it entirely. The most powerful open model you can self-host was built by a geopolitical competitor on a competing chip stack.

Meanwhile, Meta cut 8,000 jobs this week to fund $135 billion in AI infrastructure spending. Microsoft launched buyouts. Twenty thousand positions across two companies — eliminated the same week the models that replace those roles got cheaper.

If your AI strategy is still "pick the best model," you are solving last year's problem. The decision now is where it runs, who has access to the weights, and what your data touches on the way through.

It is a race. Time to catch up.
