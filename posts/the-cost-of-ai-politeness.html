The Cost of AI Politeness

"Thank you." Two tokens. Zero value. And your company just paid for it.

AI is not a person. It does not need affirmation. It does not have feelings. Every token you send is a billing event, and every billing event reprocesses the full conversation.

Here is how AI pricing actually works. Large language models do not remember your conversation. Every time you send a message, the entire history — every question you asked, every answer the model gave — gets resubmitted as input. The model reads all of it again, top to bottom, to generate the next response.

When someone on your team types "thanks!" after a 5,000-word exchange, the model reprocesses all 5,000 words. To say "You're welcome."

At $5 per million input tokens, one courtesy costs fractions of a cent. Multiply it by a thousand employees, ten times a day, across every department running AI tools. Now it is real money.

The tokens that cost you something and return nothing:

  • "Great, thanks!"
  • "OK"
  • "Perfect"
  • "Can you do that?" (after it already offered to)

The tokens that cost you nothing and return something:

  • "Rewrite this in the tone of our Q3 earnings call"
  • "Add the regional breakdown and flag anything over 10% variance"

Be direct. Be specific. Skip the pleasantries. The AI does not care. Your budget does.

It is a race. Time to catch up.
