AI Has a Trillion-Dollar Accounting Problem. Telecom Has Solved It for Forty Years.

Contributed article · by Jerry W. Lambert, Sr.

Every prompt your company sends to an AI model is a priced economic event. So is every retrieval, every tool call, every step an autonomous agent takes on your behalf. Multiply that by an enterprise's daily volume and you have a transaction economy running inside your business — one most companies cannot independently audit.

Telecom has seen this movie before. It faced exactly this problem decades ago, solved it with the Call Detail Record, and still runs that discipline every single day — billions of machine-generated transactions reconciled across competing carriers, in real time. This article argues the AI industry can inherit that discipline wholesale: an independent, per-event record kept by both buyer and provider, reconciled rather than taken on faith.
