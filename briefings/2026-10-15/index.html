Daily Model Brief, October 15, 2026

TypeSafe shipped Jev 1.14 at the same price as 1.13, while Meta's Muse Wonder, Google's Gemini 4 Argon launch price, and OpenAI's January 2027 shutdowns for GPT-4.1 and GPT-4o remain on the vendor pages.

Thursday, October 15, 2026 · Prices as of 8:26 AM CT

Download PDF

Executive Summary

TypeSafe shipped Jev 1.14 (jev-1.14.0), available from October 14, 2026 at the same price as 1.13: $0.042 per million input tokens, output free. The moving name jev-preview now resolves to 1.14; jev-latest still resolves to 1.13.

Google's Gemini 4 Argon (gemini-4-argon) is unchanged: $2.00 input, $0.10 cached and $10.00 output per million tokens through November 30, then $4.00 / $0.20 / $20.00 from December 1. Google also added an invite-only Contributor tier at half of Standard for some models; it is not a public price list add.

Meta's Muse Wonder (muse-wonder-1.0) is unchanged since it opened on the Model API on October 13, at $4.00 input, $0.20 cached and $20.00 output. Muse Spark is unchanged at $1.25 / $0.15 / $4.25.

OpenAI's January 14, 2027 shutdown for GPT-4.1 (gpt-4.1-2025-04-14), GPT-4.1 mini (gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14), GPT-4o and GPT-4o mini still stands, with gpt-6-luna as the named replacement. Prices on those models are unchanged. Shown here as Wednesday, January 13, 2027, 6:00 PM CT (00:00 UTC January 14).

Anthropic, xAI, DeepSeek, Moonshot, Z.ai, Alibaba, MiniMax, Mistral and Perplexity published no chat or token price changes. xAI renamed a voice SKU to grok-voice-3.0 at the same per-minute rate; that is off the text price list.

TypeSafe Jev 1.13 stays $0.042 input and free output. IBM Granite is unchanged at $0.0636 / $0.265 and could not be fully verified today. Prices from Amazon and ByteDance could not be confirmed today; Microsoft lists no pay-as-you-go prices and Cohere still publishes no full token matrix.

What This Means for Customers

  • If you use TypeSafe System One classifiers, jev-1.14.0 is live at the same $0.042 input / free output rate as 1.13. Point jev-preview at 1.14 if you want the newest release; keep jev-latest if you want to stay on 1.13.
  • If you want to try gemini-4-argon, the launch price still runs through November 30. Budget at $4.00 input and $20.00 output per million tokens from December 1, and remember that thinking tokens bill as output. Contributor pricing is invite-only at half of Standard for Argon and some Flash models.
  • If you call gpt-4o, gpt-4o-mini, gpt-4.1 or gpt-4.1-mini, you still have until January 14, 2027. Each GPT-4.1 model lists only one snapshot, so the plain names stop working then too. Test gpt-6-luna now; it lists at $0.10 input, $0.01 cached and $0.50 output per million tokens (short context), well below gpt-4.1 at $2.00 / $8.00.
  • OpenAI still points developers to an optional shutdown_date field on its List models and Retrieve model endpoints, so you can watch for retirement dates programmatically.
  • The October 22 and 23 dates are unchanged. gpt-4.1-nano, o4-mini and gpt-image-1 (OpenAI) shut down the evening of October 22 (CT), along with Google's gemini-omni-flash-preview and Veo 3.1 previews. Google's deep-research-pro preview shuts down October 23. Perplexity retires its routes to older OpenAI models at 7:00 PM CT on October 23.
  • Claude Haiku 4.5 is still active. Anthropic still lists retirement not sooner than today, October 15, 2026, with no date set. Keep a tested fallback ready.
